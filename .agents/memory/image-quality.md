---
name: Image quality for branded uploads
description: Preserve exact signage, logos, and text when improving uploaded website images.
---

For branded or informational images, prefer fidelity-safe programmatic upscaling and sharpening over generative enhancement when the original text and logos must remain exact.

**Why:** Generative image enhancement can increase apparent resolution while hallucinating or corrupting signage, logos, and provider names.

**How to apply:** Keep the original asset, create a high-resolution Lanczos/unsharp derivative, and verify the rendered result before replacing the website reference.
